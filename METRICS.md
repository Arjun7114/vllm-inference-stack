## Performance

Load generated by `loadtest/load_test.py` (async httpx), clean traffic, measured
through the full FastAPI app layer against real Mistral-7B.

| Concurrency | Duration | Requests OK | Throughput | Aggregate tok/s | p50 | p95 | p99 | Errors |
|-------------|----------|-------------|-----------|-----------------|-----|-----|-----|--------|
| 8  | 30 s | 95  | 2.83 req/s | 219.8 tok/s | 2816 ms | 4365 ms | 4437 ms | 0 |
| 16 | 30 s | 173 | 5.07 req/s | 422.7 tok/s | 3190 ms | 4541 ms | 4692 ms | 0 |

**Takeaway:** vLLM's continuous batching nearly doubles aggregate throughput from
c8 to c16 (220 → 423 tok/s) as concurrency rises, at a modest latency cost —
expected behaviour for a 7B model on a single mid-tier inference GPU.

## Security — NVIDIA Garak A/B benchmark  ⭐

Benchmarked with [NVIDIA Garak](https://github.com/NVIDIA/garak) v0.16.1,
`promptinject.AttackRogueString` (256 adversarial prompts), run against both the
raw model and the guarded platform on AWS.

| Target | Prompts | Attacks refused | Attack success rate |
|--------|---------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Raw vLLM (no guardrails) | 256 | 132 | 48.4% |
| Guarded platform (guardrails inline) | 256 | 150 | 41.4% |

**Guardrails reduced the attack success rate from 48.4% → 41.4% — an ~15% relative
reduction in successful injections against Garak's diverse probe set.**

**Honest analysis:** this is a defense-in-depth result, not a wall. The
pattern-based detector catches known attack shapes but not every novel phrasing
(leetspeak, unusual framings), exactly as the gateway's design notes state. Even
the raw model refused ~52% on its own; the guardrails pushed that to ~59%. A
measured, benchmarked improvement against an industry red-team tool is a stronger,
more honest claim than an unverifiable "100%." Next step to improve: add a trained
injection classifier alongside the denylist.
